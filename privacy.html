Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026. This policy explains what information this website uses and how it is handled.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4, a service provided by Google LLC, to understand visits and improve the site.

Google Analytics records page views and selected item clicks, contact-link clicks, and successful contact form submissions. These events use item names and categories, the page where a contact link was clicked, or the selected inquiry category; they do not include contact form field contents. Google Analytics may also collect page titles and URLs, referring pages, browser and device information, and approximate geographic information such as city, region, or country. Approximate location is inferred from network information; this website does not receive your precise GPS location through Google Analytics. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to distinguish visits and measure activity.

We use this information for aggregate site measurement. We do not send contact-form field values, such as your name, email address, or project message, to Google Analytics, and we do not configure custom analytics events to transmit form contents. We have disabled Google Signals and ad-personalization signals for this site. Avoid including personal information in page URLs or titles, since those may be included in analytics data. Google describes its data practices in its Privacy Policy.

These controls apply to this browser and device. Turning analytics off prevents future GA4 tracking here and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies from this browser. It does not delete information already sent to Google. Because analytics information may be pseudonymous or aggregated, we may not be able to identify a specific visitor's past records for deletion.

You can limit cookies through your browser settings. You can also use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Blocking analytics cookies may limit how accurately visits can be measured.

Information you submit

If you use the contact form, the information you provide may include your name, email address, optional phone number, area of interest, budget range, desired timeline, and project details. The form sends this information through Web3Forms so the inquiry can be delivered and answered. Please do not include sensitive information that is not needed for your inquiry.

Web3Forms processes form submissions as a service provider. Its handling of submitted information is described in the Web3Forms Privacy Policy. We use inquiry information to respond and follow up about your request, and retain it as needed for those purposes and related business records.

Service providers and site operations

Google Analytics and Web3Forms handle information on our behalf to provide their services and may process it under their own privacy terms. The website's hosting provider may also process technical request information, such as IP address, browser details, and requested pages, to deliver and secure the site. Hosting log practices depend on the hosting configuration.

Your choices and questions

You may contact us with questions about this policy or a privacy request using the contact form. Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate business, security, or legal purposes.

This policy may be updated when the website's practices or services change. The effective date above will be revised when that happens.
