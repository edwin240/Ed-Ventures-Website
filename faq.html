Custom furniture

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about commissions, materials, pricing, timelines, and care for one-of-a-kind pieces made in Maryland.

About

Where are you located?

We’re based in Silver Spring, Maryland and create custom furniture for clients in the surrounding DMV area. Availability for delivery or pickup may vary depending on the project.

What types of furniture do you make?

We create custom, one-of-a-kind furniture pieces including dining tables, coffee tables, desks, benches, and other custom wood and epoxy pieces. If you have an idea for something that isn’t listed, reach out and we can discuss it.

Custom Orders

How does the custom furniture process work?

The process starts with a conversation about what you’re looking for. From there, we can discuss dimensions, wood species, design, finish, epoxy details, and any other custom features. Inspiration photos and reference images are welcome and can help communicate your vision. Once the details are finalized, we’ll provide a quote and we can move forward with the build.

Can I send inspiration photos or examples of furniture I like?

Absolutely. Inspiration photos are encouraged. They don’t need to be exact examples of what you want. They simply help us understand your style and the details you’re drawn to, such as the shape, proportions, wood, finish, colors, or overall aesthetic. Think of it like showing a photographer examples of the lighting, mood, or style you like—the references help communicate the vision.

Can I customize the size and dimensions?

Yes. Custom dimensions are one of the advantages of commissioning a piece. We can discuss the size and proportions needed to fit your space and how you intend to use the furniture.

Can I choose the type of wood?

Yes. Wood species can be discussed based on the look, character, durability, and availability you’re looking for. Wood selection may vary depending on the project.

Can I customize the epoxy color and design?

Yes. For epoxy pieces, the color, transparency, placement, and overall design can be customized to complement the wood and the look you’re going for.

Can you make a piece based on a design I found online?

Reference images can be used to communicate the style or features you like. We can use those references as inspiration to create a custom piece tailored to your preferences, dimensions, and materials.

Pricing & Timeline

How much does a custom piece cost?

Every custom piece is priced individually. Factors such as dimensions, wood species, materials, design complexity, epoxy work, finish, and base or legs can all affect the final price. Contact us with your project details for a personalized quote.

How long does a custom piece take?

Project timelines vary depending on the size, materials, complexity, and current workload. Once the project details are discussed, we can provide an estimated timeline.

Delivery & Care

Do you offer delivery?

Delivery may be available depending on the project and location. Pickup and delivery options can be discussed when your piece is being planned.

How do I care for my custom furniture?

Care instructions will depend on the specific wood, finish, and materials used for your piece. We’ll provide guidance on how to properly clean and maintain your furniture so it continues to look great over time.

Still curious?

Tell us about your space, your ideas, or the piece you have in mind—we’d love to hear from you.
